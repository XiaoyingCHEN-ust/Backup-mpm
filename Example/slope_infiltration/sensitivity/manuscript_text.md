# Proposed manuscript revision

## Immediate wording corrections

Use “configuration-specific transition” rather than “criterion”. In the
abstract, Introduction, Section 6.4 and Conclusion, replace categorical
phrasing such as “below/above the 0.5 m threshold” with:

> For the reference geometry and parameter set, the onset-time predictions
> begin to diverge at a surface head of approximately 0.5 m.

Delete the sentence in Section 6.4 stating that the 0.5 m threshold “provides a
useful criterion” for deciding when gas evolution must be included. Also
replace “systematic rather than case-specific” with “systematic within the
reference parameter set”.

## Limitation paragraph

> The sensitivity study is intentionally limited and does not define a
> transferable critical head. Initial saturation and the SWRC parameters
> control gas-filled volume and capillary storage, while slope geometry and
> strength parameters control proximity to localization; each can shift the
> onset-time curves. Gas venting, partial ponding and mixed hydraulic
> boundaries may likewise weaken or redistribute the gas-pressure response.
> Accordingly, the approximately 0.5 m transition observed in the reference
> case should not be extrapolated directly to other slopes.
